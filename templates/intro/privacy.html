Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

AuraPOS operates as a software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider for salon billing, appointment scheduling, and customer management. We are committed to protecting the privacy of salon owners, stylists, and clients.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Salon name, owner contact details, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Billing & Transaction Data: Service orders, staff commission allocations, and invoice histories processed through our register.
  • Payment Processing: Online subscription payments are tokenized and securely handled via Razorpay. We do not store raw card numbers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All communications are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Database snapshots are encrypted at rest with regular security audits.

4. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding data retention or would like to request account deletion, please email us at salonbillingsoftware@gmail.com.
