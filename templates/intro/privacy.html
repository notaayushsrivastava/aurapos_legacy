Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your data while using Salon Billing Software.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you create an account or use our software:

  • Salon name and owner details
  • Email address and phone number
  • Billing and subscription information
  • Customer and appointment data you enter
  • Service and inventory records

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our services.
  • To process subscriptions and payments.
  • To maintain customer records and billing history.
  • To communicate important account notifications.
  • To provide technical support.

3. Payment Information

Payments are securely processed through Razorpay. We do not store your credit card, debit card, UPI PIN, or other sensitive payment credentials on our servers.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may only be shared with trusted service providers necessary for operating the software, such as payment processors or hosting providers, or when required by applicable law.

6. Your Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and ensuring the accuracy of the information you provide.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us using the details provided on the Contact page.
